## What it is
- ecoDMS: document management and digital archiving program from ecoDMS GmbH (Aachen). Central storage of documents, receipts and emails, full-text search with OCR, GoBD and DSGVO compliance, workflows via WORKZ add-on, e-invoice archiving, API (unlimited in ecoDMS ONE). Also ecoMAILZ (email archiving) and ecoDMS ONE.
- Pricing hints: from 89 EUR incl. VAT (ecoDMS), 69 EUR (ecoMAILZ), 250 EUR net (ecoDMS ONE); free trial.
