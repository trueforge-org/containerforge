# NextPVR wiki home

**Source:** https://github.com/sub3/NextPVR/wiki (fetched 2026-11-26)

## What it is
- Personal video recorder for live TV: series recording, web scheduling, iOS client, Kodi/Emby integration.
- Closed source. Free for personal non-commercial use; commercial use needs a license (nextpvr.com).
- Platforms: Windows, Linux, Mac, Docker.
- Tuner sources: IPTV, HDHomeRun, SAT>IP, DVB/ATSC (Docker supports all of these).
- EPG: SAT>IP/DVB broadcast data; IPTV XMLTV; Schedules Direct and XMLTV supported.
- Third-party: FFmpeg, TVDB, TMDB, Fanart.tv.
